What about the hardware?

Bring what you have. Mezapos runs in a browser — any Android tablet for the waiters and cashier, any old laptop for the kitchen screen, any phone for the guest. The only thing we send you is the QR sheet, at print cost.

How long does setup take?

One afternoon. We sit with you on-site, import your menu, set up the stations, send the QR sheet to your printer, and walk your team through the tablets. You open on Friday like always — just lighter.

What do guests pay on?

M-Pesa, card, or both — through kalatas. The rates are passed through at cost; Mezapos doesn't mark them up. Your existing M-Pesa till keeps working; the card payments settle to the bank account you already use.

And eTIMS?

Handled. Every receipt is reported to KRA the moment it's settled, and your Z-report is generated automatically at end-of-shift. The eTIMS spreadsheet you've been keeping by hand can go in the bin.

What if Wi-Fi goes down?

The tablets keep taking orders offline. The KDS keeps firing tickets. Once the connection is back, everything syncs in the background — including payments that were queued. Your guests don't see any of this.

Can I cancel?

Monthly. Switch off any time; you'll be able to export your menu and your historical orders before you go. Karibu tena.
